    // 02 · The thesis

    The asset you mail beats the words you send.

    // core principle

    We market to the asset, not the human.

    Traditional marketing guesses at people: personas, demographics, emotional hooks. We don't profile owners. We price properties. Each address is treated like an instrument, valued on its own fundamentals, and the mail goes where the asset says the deal is.

    Human-first marketing
    • Personas and demographic guesses
    • Copy written to trigger emotion
    • Success depends on the ad
    Asset-first marketing
    • Property-level signals: value, age, tenure, occupancy, location
    • Every address priced by expected value
    • Success depends on the math

    Direct mail has three levers. Most marketers pour their time into the one that matters least: rewriting copy and redesigning postcards. We work them in order of impact.

    01

    Segmentation

    Primary edge

    Which assets you mail. Every property priced by expected value, every market by crowding. Get this wrong and no headline saves the campaign. Get it right and even plain mail performs.

    02

    Frequency

    Compounding layer

    When and how often. Cumulative touches and cadence change who answers and how good the deal is. Tuning the sequence is its own layer of edge, and most senders leave it on autopilot.

    03

    Creative

    Test last

    What it says. It matters at the margin, so we treat it like any other variable: hold the segment constant, A/B test the copy, and keep the winner. Never the other way around.

    nail segmentation→layer frequency→A/B test creative

    // 05 · Strategy

    Run your mail like a book, not a lottery.

    Edge comes from process, not luck: price everything, size positions to the edge, fade crowded trades, and keep re-fitting as the market adapts.

    01 / PRICE

    Price every property

    Each address gets an expected value per piece, not a yes/no filter. Mailing is a bet; we tell you the odds.

    02 / SIZE

    Size the position

    Allocate budget across counties and segments by edge. Size up where it pays, zero out where it doesn't.

    03 / AVOID CROWDING

    Fade the crowd

    When everyone mails the same list, the spread disappears. Crowding scores steer you to markets that haven't been picked over.

    04 / RE-FIT

    Manage signal decay

    Edges fade as competitors adapt. Models get re-scored on fresh data so your list doesn't go stale.

    Discretionary mailingPull-and-pray

    • Broad filters like "absentee, 20+ years owned"
    • Same crowded list every competitor mails
    • Judged on last month's luck, not the odds
    • Budget spread evenly, edge or not
    • Weeks spent polishing copy for the wrong list

    Systematic mailingdairectmail.ai

    • Every property priced by expected value
    • Positions taken where crowding is low
    • Judged on process and long-run EV, not variance
    • Budget sized to edge, market by market
    • Segmentation first, then frequency, then tested creative
